IMPORTANT LIMITATION
Browser Wake Lock can help prevent normal screen sleep while the page is open, but it cannot guarantee research continues if the computer actually sleeps, hibernates, the lid is closed, the browser is killed, or the network is suspended. For true 24/7 collection, move the tick collector to an always-on server/background worker (for example Render) and let the browser dashboard read the stored research data.

STORAGE
The lab still retains up to 30,000 scored records locally. Long-running research beyond that should be moved to server-side or IndexedDB storage so old forward observations are not dropped from detailed analysis.
