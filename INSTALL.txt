WHAT THIS ADDS

The dashboard now distinguishes the exact trade lifecycle:

Prediction Source
  The canonical tick/epoch whose completed history produced the target.

Active Target
  The digit actually attached to the purchased contract.

Eligible Result Tick
  The first SERVER tick whose epoch is later than the contract's armed epoch.

Comparison
  Example:
      6 = 6  -> WIN
      6 != 4 -> LOSS

It also shows:
- contract id
- trade number
- armed-after epoch
- fast result
- official Deriv result once the same contract settles

WHY THIS MATTERS

A visible Current Tick digit is not automatically the result tick for the
contract. The result tick must be the first eligible server tick AFTER the
contract was armed.

This panel makes that distinction explicit.

EXPECTED EXAMPLE

Prediction Source:
  e:1791531000 · digit 4

Active Target:
  6

Eligible Result Tick:
  e:1791531001 · digit 6

Comparison:
  6 = 6

Verdict:
  WIN · DERIV WIN

If target 6 and the eligible result digit is 6 but the official Deriv result
is LOSS, that is a genuine mismatch worth investigating.
