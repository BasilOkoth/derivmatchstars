Connect with Deriv using OAuth. Your Deriv access token is kept encrypted on the DigitMatchStar server, rather than stored in the browser.

HOW THE SECURE FLOW WORKS
1. You approve DigitMatchStar on Deriv.
2. Deriv returns authorization to the backend.
3. Your Deriv token is encrypted in Postgres.
4. Your browser receives only a DigitMatchStar session token.
5. Your server-side trading session is isolated to your account.
