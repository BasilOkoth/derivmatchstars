TICK-DRIVEN DEMO STRATEGY FIX

This implements the requested behavior:

Example target digit: 5

1. Buy a 1-tick DIGITMATCH contract for digit 5.
2. Keep the next recovery proposal pre-armed.
3. Watch the live tick stream.
4. On the first eligible tick after the BUY:
   - last digit == 5 -> FAST_WON, stop the strategy immediately.
   - last digit != 5 -> FAST loss, advance immediately to the next recovery.
5. Deriv proposal_open_contract settlement continues independently in the
   background and updates authoritative P/L/accounting only.

Important architecture changes:
- Strategy progression no longer waits for proposal_open_contract.is_sold.
- Settlement subscriptions are keyed by (session_id, contract_id), so an older
  contract can reconcile in the background while the next strategy contract is
  already active.
- Official settlement never clears a newer open_contract_id.
- Repeated sold events are accounted exactly once using TradeLog.status.
- Recovery proposal is prefetched before settlement subscription setup.
- The live WebSocket reader never awaits business callbacks.
- A cached-tick replay handles the race where the settlement tick arrives while
  the BUY response is still in flight; this prevents the classic one-tick-late
  behavior.
- A fast-result vs official-result disagreement stops the strategy with
  RECONCILE_MISMATCH rather than cascading from an incorrectly correlated tick.

Replace all four files:
  app/engine.py
  app/deriv_ws.py
  backend/app/engine.py
  backend/app/deriv_ws.py

Then restart the backend service.
