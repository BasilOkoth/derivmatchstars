WHAT CHANGED IN v1.1
- New clean cohort storage, separate from v1.0.
- One cohort is frozen and survives normal reloads/redeploy refreshes through dedicated localStorage cohort metadata.
- Cohort ID, historical-data hash, candidate-set hash, discovery start/end epochs, and forward-start epoch are exported.
- Reload does NOT rediscover candidates.
- Only the explicit NEW RESEARCH COHORT button can intentionally reset/re-discover candidates.
- Duplicate/overlapping tick epochs are rejected.
- Discovery history is loaded before the forward subscription starts, preventing history/live overlap.
- On feed gaps/reconnects, a small feature-only warmup is used; it does not alter candidate definitions or forward statistics.
- Logical candidate deduplication removes redundant states such as digit parity duplicates and last-5-all-even versus EVEN-run-5+ aliases.
- Leaderboard is ranked from forward evidence, not historical discovery percentage.
- Candidate statuses: FORWARD TESTING, STATISTICALLY CLEARED, REJECTED.
- Dedicated small cohort/progress stores reduce the risk that a large record log prevents the frozen registry from persisting.
- REAL still never auto-resumes after reload.
- Admin-only access checks, screen wake lock, draggable/minimizable panel, SHADOW/DEMO/REAL, and manual/auto execution remain.
