PRIVACY BEHAVIOR
- The Under-9 panel does not render until /api/capture-access authorizes the logged-in Deriv account/token.
- Unauthorized visitors do not get the panel or the Under-9 public-tick research connection.
- DEMO/REAL execution also rechecks admin authorization before trading.
- Authorization is rechecked every 60 seconds; loss of authorization hides/stops the lab.
- The JavaScript file itself can still be downloaded by a technically sophisticated visitor because it is a public web asset. Privacy/security comes from the server-side /api/capture-access decision, not from hiding JavaScript.
